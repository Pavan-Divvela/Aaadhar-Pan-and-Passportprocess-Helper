# Passport Seva (Ministry of External Affairs) Guidelines

## 1. Overview of Passport Services
Indian passports are issued by the CPV (Consular, Passport and Visa) Division of the Ministry of External Affairs through Passport Seva Kendras (PSK) and Post Office Passport Seva Kendras (POPSK).

## 2. Normal vs. Tatkaal Scheme
* **Normal Scheme:**
  * Fee: ₹1,500 (36 pages booklet) / ₹2,000 (60 pages jumbo booklet).
  * Validity: 10 years for adults (5 years or until age 18 for minors).
  * Police verification occurs before passport dispatch (Pre-Police Verification).
  * Turnaround: 15 to 30 working days.
* **Tatkaal Scheme:**
  * Fee: Additional ₹2,000 over Normal fee (Total ₹3,500 for 36 pages / ₹4,000 for 60 pages).
  * Issuance without waiting for police verification (Post-Police Verification or No Verification).
  * Dispatched within 1 to 3 working days after appointment.
  * Strict Document Requirements: Must provide 3 identity proofs from the approved list (e.g. Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, Bank Passbook, Service ID). Names must match 100%.

## 3. Non-ECR (Emigration Check Not Required)
* Passports in India are either marked with **ECR** (Emigration Check Required) or **Non-ECR**.
* Non-ECR allows travel without clearance for work to 18 countries (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Malaysia, etc.).
* **Who qualifies for Non-ECR?**
  * All persons having educational qualifications of Matriculation (10th standard) or above.
  * All Income Tax Assessees (taxpayers who filed ITR in personal capacity).
  * All persons above 50 years of age.
  * Children below 18 years of age.
  * Spouses of persons holding Non-ECR status.

## 4. Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) Appointment Tips
* **Color Original Documents:** Always carry original documents plus 2 sets of self-attested photocopies.
* **Photograph:** For adults, photos are captured live digitally at the PSK counter. You do not need physical photos unless applying for children under 4 years old.
* **Counter Navigation:** PSK has Counters A (Biometrics & photo), B (Document verification by TCS/MEA officers), and C (Granting officer interview).

## 5. Police Verification Preparation
* The local police station beat officer will call or visit your registered present address.
* Keep copies ready: Aadhaar, Electricity Bill/Rent Agreement, 2 neighbor references with their Aadhaar copies and phone numbers.
