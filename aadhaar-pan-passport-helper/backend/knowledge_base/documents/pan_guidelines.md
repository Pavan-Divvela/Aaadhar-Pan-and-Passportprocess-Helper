# Income Tax Department PAN Card Guidelines

## 1. Permanent Account Number (PAN) Overview
PAN is a 10-digit alphanumeric identifier issued by the Income Tax Department under Section 139A. It is mandatory for tax filing, opening bank accounts, high-value transactions, and investing in stocks/mutual funds.

## 2. Instant e-PAN vs Form 49A
* **Instant e-PAN via Aadhaar e-KYC:**
  * Applicable for applicants who have never held a PAN.
  * Applicant must possess an Aadhaar card linked with an active mobile number.
  * Issued within 10 to 30 minutes in digitally signed PDF format.
  * 100% Free of charge.
  * Physical reprint can be ordered later from Protean or UTIITSL for ₹50.
* **Form 49A (New Physical PAN):**
  * Applied through Protean (formerly NSDL e-Gov) or UTIITSL.
  * Fee: ₹107 for dispatch within India, ₹1,017 for dispatch outside India.
  * Required if applicant has no Aadhaar or wishes to provide custom signature and photo.

## 3. Mandatory PAN-Aadhaar Linking Rules
* Under Section 139AA and Section 234H of the Income Tax Act, linking PAN with Aadhaar is mandatory for all eligible citizens.
* **Inoperative PAN Consequences:** Unlinked PAN cards become inoperative. Inoperative PAN cannot be used for income tax refunds, higher TDS is deducted (up to 20%), and pending ITRs are not processed.
* **Penalty Payment Flow:**
  1. Visit Income Tax e-Filing portal -> e-Pay Tax.
  2. Enter PAN and mobile for OTP verification.
  3. Select Income Tax tile (Payment Code: Major Head 0021 / Minor Head 500 - Other Receipts / Fee for linking).
  4. Pay ₹1,000 via Netbanking, UPI, or Debit Card.
  5. Wait 24 to 48 hours for Challan to reflect.
  6. Return to "Link Aadhaar" page and submit linking request.

## 4. Name & Initial Rules on PAN
* The Income Tax Department **strictly does NOT allow initials** in full names or father's name.
* If your name on school documents is "R. Karthik", PAN requires full expansion (e.g. "Ramachandran Karthik").
* If Aadhaar contains an initial ("R. Karthik") and PAN contains expanded name ("Ramachandran Karthik"), linking will fail with a demographic mismatch.
* Solution: First update Aadhaar to match the expanded format, then link.
