# UIDAI Aadhaar Comprehensive Guidelines & Rules

## 1. Enrollment Overview
Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique random identification number issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI). Aadhaar enrollment is completely free of charge. Every resident Indian citizen, regardless of age, can enroll.

## 2. Enrollment Procedure
* **Biometrics Required:** For applicants aged 5 years and above, ten fingerprints, both iris scans, and a facial photograph are captured.
* **Mandatory Biometric Updates (MBU):**
  * Children must update biometrics upon reaching **Age 5** and **Age 15**.
  * Both mandatory updates (Age 5 and Age 15) are **100% FREE** of cost at all Aadhaar centers.
* **Adult Biometrics:** Required if biometrics are failing during authentication (e.g. fingerprint wear in manual labor or elderly citizens). Fee is ₹100.

## 3. Demographic Updates & Lifetime Limits
UIDAI imposes strict lifetime limits to prevent identity fraud:
* **Name Change:** Allowed only **2 times** in a lifetime. Requires valid Proof of Identity (PoI) such as Passport, Voter ID, or 10th Board Certificate.
* **Date of Birth (DoB) Change:** Allowed only **1 time** in a lifetime. Can only be updated if the existing date was unverified. Requires Birth Certificate or Board Marksheet.
* **Gender Change:** Allowed only **1 time** in a lifetime.
* **Address Update:** No lifetime limit. Can be updated online through the myAadhaar portal using a valid Proof of Address (PoA) or Head of Family (HoF) based confirmation.

## 4. Acceptable Documents
* **Proof of Identity (PoI):** Voter ID, Driving Licence, Indian Passport, PAN Card, Ration Card with photo, 10th Marks Sheet with photo.
* **Proof of Address (PoA):** Electricity Bill (last 3 months), Water Bill, Bank Passbook with branch stamp and photo, Registered Rent Agreement, Gas Connection Bill, MP/MLA Certificate in UIDAI format.
* **Proof of Date of Birth (PoB):** Birth Certificate, 10th School Leaving Certificate, Passport.

## 5. PVC Card & Masked Aadhaar
* **Aadhaar PVC Card:** Can be ordered for ₹50 on myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in. Includes hologram, microtext, and ghost image.
* **Masked Aadhaar:** Displays only the last 4 digits (e.g., `XXXX-XXXX-1234`). Masked Aadhaar is legally valid for hotel check-ins, KYC, and travel, preventing identity theft.
