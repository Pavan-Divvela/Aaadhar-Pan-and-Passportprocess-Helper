# Document Discrepancy & Name Mismatch Resolution Guide

## 1. Golden Hierarchy of Documents
When personal details differ across your records, Indian authorities follow this legal authority hierarchy:
1. **Supreme Root Document:** Birth Certificate or 10th Standard / Matriculation Board Marksheet.
2. **Primary Identity Anchor:** Aadhaar Card (align this with your 10th Board Certificate).
3. **Financial Identifier:** PAN Card (align this with your Aadhaar Card).
4. **International Travel Document:** Passport (issued based on 10th cert + Aadhaar + PAN alignment).

## 2. Common Mismatches & Solutions

### A. Initial vs. Expanded Full Name (e.g. "K. Sharma" vs "Kumar Sharma")
* Aadhaar accepts initials, but PAN and Passport require full names.
* **Resolution Path:**
  1. Take your 10th Certificate showing full name.
  2. Visit an Aadhaar Seva Kendra or apply online to expand initial in Aadhaar.
  3. Once updated Aadhaar is generated, update PAN.
  4. Finally link PAN-Aadhaar or apply for Passport.

### B. Single Name / No Surname (e.g. "Pooja")
* Having only one name causes major international travel issues:
  * US and European visas often print "FNU" (First Name Unknown) or refuse visa issuance.
  * In Indian passports, if you put "Pooja" in Given Name, the system moves it to Surname and leaves Given Name empty.
* **Resolution Path:**
  * Add your family surname or father's name to Aadhaar first using an affidavit or gazette notice before applying for a fresh passport.

### C. Marriage Surname Change for Women
* Many women change surname from maiden name to married surname (e.g., "Sharma" to "Verma").
* **Passport Seva Rule:** Marriage certificate is **NOT mandatory** to add spouse name or change surname on passport. A simple self-declaration in standard Annexure D is accepted.
* However, updating Aadhaar address and surname first with marriage certificate makes all subsequent bank, PAN, and passport updates 10x smoother.

### D. Major Name Change (Complete Rename)
* If changing name completely (e.g., from "Rajesh Gupta" to "Arjun Kapoor"):
  1. Prepare an Affidavit on ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper attested by a First Class Judicial Magistrate or Notary.
  2. Publish advertisements in two daily newspapers (one English national daily, one vernacular local daily).
  3. Apply for publication in the Gazette of India (Department of Publication, Civil Lines, Delhi).
  4. Submit Gazette copy to UIDAI and Passport Seva.
