Ministry of External Affairs Guide

Indian Passport Seva Guide

Complete instructions for Fresh & Re-issue passports, Tatkaal eligibility, Non-ECR status, and PSK appointment success.

📘

Normal Scheme

Standard issuance with pre-police verification.

Official Fee: ₹1,500 (36 pages) / ₹2,000 (60 pages)

Timeline: 15 to 30 working days

Documents: Standard Proof of Present Address (PoA) + Date of Birth (PoB) + Non-ECR proof

⚡

Tatkaal Scheme (Urgent)

Dispatched within 1 to 3 days. Police verification happens after dispatch.

Official Fee: ₹3,500 (36 pages) / ₹4,000 (60 pages)

Timeline: 1 to 3 working days

Documents: 3 distinct proofs from approved 16-doc list (Aadhaar + PAN + Voter ID). Zero name discrepancy permitted.

🏢 What Happens at the Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)?

Arrive 15 minutes prior to your appointment time with original documents plus 2 self-attested photocopies of each proof. You will navigate three sequential counters:

Counter A (TCS Staff)

Digital photograph capture, biometric fingerprint scanning, and high-resolution scanning of original documents.

Counter B (Verification Officer)

Government officer physically inspects original documents, matches spellings, and verifies educational certificates for Non-ECR.

Counter C (Granting Officer)

Assistant Passport Officer conducts the final interview, approves or marks for review, and grants passport issuance.
