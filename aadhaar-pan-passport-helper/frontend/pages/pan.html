🔗 How to Link PAN with Aadhaar (Step-by-Step)

Linking is mandatory under Section 139AA. If your PAN has become inoperative, higher TDS (up to 20%) will apply and tax refunds will be withheld.

Step 1: Check Current Link Status

Visit the e-Filing portal -> Click "Link Aadhaar Status". Enter your 10-digit PAN and 12-digit Aadhaar. If already linked, no fee or further action is required.

Step 2: Pay ₹1,000 Late Fee Challan

Go to e-Pay Tax. Enter PAN and mobile OTP. Under the Income Tax tile, select:
• Assessment Year: Current Year
• Type of Payment: Fee for delay in linking PAN with Aadhaar (Minor Head 500)
Pay ₹1,000 via Netbanking, Debit Card, or UPI.

Step 3: Wait 24 to 48 Hours for Challan Clearance

Payment verification does not happen instantly. Do not attempt to re-pay! Wait 24–48 hours until the Income Tax portal recognizes the BSR code and Challan number.

Step 4: Submit Final Linking Request

Return to "Link Aadhaar". The system will show "Your payment details are verified". Click Continue and verify the 6-digit Aadhaar OTP to finalize.

⚠️ Getting "Demographic Mismatch" Error during Linking?

The Income Tax Department strictly verifies exact spelling and Date of Birth against UIDAI. If your PAN has expanded names (*Ramesh Kumar Sharma*) and Aadhaar has initials (*R. K. Sharma*), automated verification will fail.
