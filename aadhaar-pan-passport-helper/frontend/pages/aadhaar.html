Aadhaar Enrollment & Update Guide

Complete instructions for first-time enrollment, demographic corrections, mandatory childhood biometric updates, and ordering durable PVC cards.

👶

Mandatory Biometrics

Children must update fingerprints and iris scans at Age 5 and Age 15. Both updates are completely FREE.

⏳

Lifetime Limits

UIDAI limits: Name: 2 times, DoB: 1 time, Gender: 1 time. Address has unlimited updates with valid proof.

🛡️

Masked Aadhaar

Displays only the last 4 digits (e.g. XXXX-XXXX-1234). 100% legally valid for hotel check-ins and travel KYC to prevent identity misuse.
